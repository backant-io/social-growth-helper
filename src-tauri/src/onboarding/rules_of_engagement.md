# Rules of engagement

This app is a guideline and a tool. You are the author and the owner of everything that goes out under your name.

- Do not spam. No copy-paste replies, no mass following, no engagement farming, no tagging strangers for reach.
- Do not post AI slop. Read every draft, rewrite what does not sound like you, and delete what adds nothing. If you would not say it out loud, do not post it.
- Nothing is posted for you. Every reply, quote, like, repost, bookmark and follow happens only after you click it yourself.
- You are responsible for staying within the rules of X, Anthropic and xAI. Using a real browser does not make automated or high-volume behaviour allowed; the app may help you move faster, but the pace and the judgement are yours.
- If X shows a challenge, a warning or a rate limit, stop and handle it by hand.

Scout, Publish and every write action stay locked until you acknowledge this notice.
